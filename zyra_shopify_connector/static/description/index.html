Shopify orders into Odoo, done right.

What you get
01
Multi-store
Connect several Shopify stores. Each one has its own company, warehouse, pricelist, sales team and salesperson.
02
Flexible order import
Import by date range, order ID or last sync. Run it manually or on a schedule, with overlap protection.
03
Smart customers
Matched by saved mapping, Shopify ID, email, then phone. Guest checkout and reusable billing and shipping contacts.
04
Accurate products
Matched by variant ID, SKU, attributes, then barcode. Never by name. Unmatched lines wait for review.
05
Reliable totals
Discounts, shipping, taxes and payment gateway are imported. Shopify and Odoo totals are compared against your tolerance.
06
Safe and recoverable
No duplicate orders. A failed order never stops the run, and the retry queue and plain-language logs help you fix it.
